Understanding Stress Levels

Stress in Modern Life

Academic pressure, work expectations, financial responsibilities, and constant digital engagement have increased stress globally. Students and professionals commonly report fatigue, anxiety, and reduced work-life balance.

Factors That Increase Stress

  • Excessive workload or deadlines
  • Poor sleep and irregular routine
  • Social isolation
  • Financial uncertainty
  • Long screen exposure

Factors That Reduce Stress

  • Regular physical movement
  • Supportive social interaction
  • Balanced daily schedule
  • Relaxation practices
  • Healthy sleep patterns
