Understand and Manage Your Stress

Stress Bar is a simple self-assessment platform that helps you understand your current stress level based on daily habits, emotional patterns, and lifestyle factors.

By answering a short questionnaire, you receive a personalized stress score and guidance to improve your wellbeing.

Why Measure Stress?

Continuous stress affects concentration, sleep, emotional balance, and physical health. Identifying stress early helps prevent burnout, anxiety, and fatigue.

How Stress Bar Works

The assessment evaluates workload, emotional stability, sleep quality, and physical symptoms to determine your stress level. Results provide insight into whether your stress is low, moderate, or high.
