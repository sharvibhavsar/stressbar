Ways to Manage Stress

Immediate Relief

  • Pause and take slow breaths
  • Step away briefly from work
  • Stretch or walk for a few minutes
  • Listen to calming audio

Daily Balance Habits

  • Maintain consistent sleep timing
  • Include light physical activity
  • Plan realistic daily goals
  • Take scheduled breaks

Emotional Wellbeing

  • Share concerns with trusted people
  • Write thoughts in a journal
  • Practice gratitude reflection
  • Limit negative self-talk

Digital Lifestyle

  • Reduce unnecessary screen exposure
  • Avoid devices before sleep
  • Schedule offline time daily
